Telco customer churn prediction project using SQL, Python (Pandas), and Machine Learning (Scikit-Learn). Dataset: "Telco Customer Churn" Kaggle.

1. Overall Churn Rate: Calculating total customers and baseline churn percentage across the organization.
2. Tenure & Contract Analysis: Identifying customer churn rates based on tenure length and subscription contract types.
3. Churn Prediction Modeling: Building a machine learning classifier to accurately predict customer churn risk.

Database: SQLite  
Language & Libraries: Python, Pandas, SQLite3, Scikit-Learn

Summary of Insights:
1. Contract Impact: Customers on Month-to-Month contracts have the highest churn rate compared to those on 1-year or 2-year commitments.
2. Tenure Correlation: Shorter tenure customers are significantly more prone to churn, highlighting critical early-stage retention needs.
3. Predictive Accuracy: Successfully trained a Random Forest Classifier model to predict customer churn risk for proactive business decision-making.
